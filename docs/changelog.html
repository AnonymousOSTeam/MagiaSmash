v0.1.0

First release Playable prototype

The first playable build: a complete five-wave arena prototype.

  • Player combat — move, jump, dash, a three-hit light chain and a heavy attack, with startup/active/recovery frames, hitstop, hitstun, lunge and directional knockback.
  • Enemies — Chiropteran and the tougher BigChiropteran, with data-driven AI attack range and delay.
  • Waves — five escalating waves spawned from alternating sides, with per-wave difficulty scaling; health carries between waves.
  • Match flow — victory on clearing the final wave, defeat on losing all health, and instant restart.
  • Feel — hit sparks, slash arcs, dash pulse, camera shake and SDF-glow UI text.
  • HUD — health bar, wave label, enemy counter and centred messages.
  • Architecture — a single C++ Fighter system for all combatants, a pocketpy game director, and fully data-driven INI content bridged through [MatchState].
